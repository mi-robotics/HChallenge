Overview

How the flow goes into Cosmos

  • Cosmos only takes in and predicts latent video frames, each a fixed block of 16 × 32 × 56 = 28,672 numbers. The flow is 896 numbers.
  • To predict flow without changing the model, we write the flow into one extra latent frame, repeated 32 times to fill it, and put it after the video frames (the Cosmos Policy trick).
  • Cosmos treats it like any other frame: it is noised, cut into tokens and denoised with the video, so the hand's motion and the video are predicted together. We read the flow back by averaging the 32 copies.
  • The noise is the same for every copy. With different noise per copy, the model could clean the flow by averaging its copies without looking at the image, and would learn to predict the average flow.
  • The flow state (v2) uses the same trick: one row of 56 numbers, the hand and mug now, fills its own frame.

End-to-end evaluation

Results

  • Two flow-conditioned policies from the Policy page (one table each), with the same eval: closed loop in the sim, 10 actions per prediction. Within a table only the flow differs: ground truth, or the WAM's plan from the first real frame. The same WAM plans are used for both policies.
  • Test (val + test, 20 episodes): 5 WAM plans per episode (plan seeds) × 5 policy seeds = 25 runs. Train and failed: 1 plan × 5 policy seeds. Success rate (%), mean ± std over the runs. The baseline and ground truth flow rows are the Policy page's evals (5 seeds × 5 attempts).
  • Flow state: v1 has none; v2 predicts it in the first chunk, or is given the ground truth flow row at frame 0 (ablation).
  • Pauses: the WAM's plans rarely hold still, so the replay's pauses are inserted after planning: the flow holds 1.0 s at the grasp and 0.75 s at the release, found from the plan's thumb and index tips.
