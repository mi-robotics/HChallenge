Step 4.4 · Phase 4

Hand and mug flow for all 163 episodes, kept and failed

  • flow_sim.npz: the flow re-timed onto the robot's clock, one row per sim.npz step, so flow and robot data have the same length (median 177 steps, 8.85 s; flow.npz on the real clock is 6.1 s). The 55 steps per episode that the replay inserts hold the flow still. A sample at sim step i is rows i, i+2, …, i+30 (16 steps at 10 Hz of robot time).
  • The hand flow's pinch point (thumb–index midpoint), moved into the sim world, is within 2 mm (median; max 2.3 cm) of the target the robot was sent while carrying, over all 100 kept episodes: the flow is the robot's path.
  • The gripper trails that target by about 0.2 s (4 control steps): gripper vs the target at the same step, median 5.4 cm while carrying; with the 0.2 s lag removed, 1.0 cm (p90 3.0 cm).
  • The mug flow does not land on the sim cup (median 6.9 cm apart in xy at the start): the offset is set so the pinch lands on the sim grasp spot (cup centre + 4 cm in y), but I grasp the real mug at different spots around the rim (the 4.2 limitation).
