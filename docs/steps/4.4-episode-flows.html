  • The 2 failed episodes without flow have no hand track (MediaPipe hung on them in step 4.2).
  • The mug is now found automatically: SAM 2's mask generator on the first frame, filtered for mug-shaped masks, nearest the hand's first pinch (last frame in 3 episodes where the mug was not found in the first). Step 3.4 used a click.
  • Grasp and release times come from when the mug starts and stops moving, not from the hand, so failed episodes are timed right: 12 of them have no grasp from the hand track at all, and in others the hand events are off by up to 3.8 s. In kept episodes the mug starts moving a median 0.54 s after the pinch closes and stops 0.73 s before the hand opens.
  • Low mug visibility is the hand covering the mug while carrying; hidden points are interpolated and flagged (red in the video).
