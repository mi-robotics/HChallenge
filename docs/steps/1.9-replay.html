Rim-grasp constraints

I pinch the mug from the side. The Panda can't: the mug (9–10 cm) is wider than its 8 cm grip, so it grasps the rim with one finger inside the mug, 4 cm from the centre. Followed exactly, my hand path drives that inside finger through the mug wall on the way in and on the way out. Three changes to the commanded path fix this. Each one only acts near the grasp or the release; everywhere else the gripper follows the hand path.

ConstraintRuleWithout it
Approach Until the grasp, the target can't go below a height floor: 8 cm above the grasp point while the gripper is ≥ 6 cm away horizontally, falling linearly to 0 at 3 cm. The distance is measured from where the gripper is, not from its target, because the arm lags fast hand motion by up to 10 cm. (A first version that used the target got 3 / 5.) Demo 3: the gripper comes in 2–3 cm above grasp height and hits the mug 1.2 cm short of the grasp point with the fingers still open. The mug is pushed 4 cm and tips, and ends 39.9 cm from the plate.
Settle At the grasp, insert 1 s: hold the grasp point for 0.5 s with the fingers open, then 0.5 s while closing. After that the hand path resumes (shifted 1 s later). Demo 5: the fingers close while the gripper is still resting on the rim, 3.2 cm above the grasp point, and my hand starts lifting 0.4 s later. The fingers close on air: the cup lifts 0.5 cm and stays on the table.
Lift out After the fingers open: hold 0.25 s, rise 10 cm straight up (1 cm per step), then follow the hand path again, never lower than 10 cm above the release point. Demo 2: after letting go, the gripper moves sideways only 2–3 cm higher. The finger still inside the mug drags it to 3.8 cm from the plate centre, and LIBERO's On(cup, plate) check fails.

Ablation: every subset of the three

All 5 demos replayed with each of the 8 subsets of constraints. The constraints work together. Approach alone fixes demo 3 but breaks the grasp in demo 5: the gripper arrives later, so the hand's close command fires before it is in place, and settle is needed to make it wait. Removing any one constraint loses at least one demo.
