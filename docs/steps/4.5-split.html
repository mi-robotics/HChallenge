  • test_novel: the 10 kept episodes with the cup furthest right. The line is tight: the nearest train episode is 2 mm inside it. train / val / test: the other 90, shuffled with seed 0.
  • The held-out region is also where replays fail most: 18 of the 63 failed episodes vs 10 of the 100 kept. There the cup is 0.8–1 m from the camera, where D405 depth reads 2–4% short.
  • The sim cup always starts at one spot, so for the sim robot test_novel means new absolute flow positions and plate offsets, not a new cup spot in sim.
  • Below: where the data lives, why episodes were dropped, frames and clocks, and every file and array in an episode folder (same as data/README.md).

Frames and clocks

  • Table frame: ArUco marker (id 0) at the origin, x away from the camera across the table, y left, z up, metres. Table axes = sim world axes.
  • Table → sim world: p_sim = p_table + sim_minus_real (sim.npz), one translation per episode that puts the pinch at the moment of grasp on the sim rim-grasp point. It does not put the mug flow on the sim cup (median 6.9 cm off, because I grasp the real rim at different spots).
  • Clocks: camera 30 fps; traj/retarget 20 Hz on the real clock; flow.npz 10 Hz on the real clock; sim.npz and flow_sim.npz 20 Hz on the robot's clock. A sim step follows the real video at t_real; 55 steps per episode are inserted by the replay (grasp settle and close, release hold and rise, final 1 s wait), and t_real holds during them. All t are seconds since the first camera frame.
