Step 4.5 · Phase 4

Dataset: paired video, flow and robot data, split 80 / 10 / 10

Training data: three files per episode, matched step for step

Everything for training is on the robot's clock: one row (or video frame) per sim control step, 20 Hz.

filecontents
video_sim.mp4the real colour video with the pauses: frame i = the camera frame sim step i follows (1280 × 720, 20 fps)
flow_sim.npzhand and mug flow with the pauses: row i = the flow at that same moment
sim.npzrobot data: action, proprioception, end-effector pose, object state, third-person and gripper camera images

Same length in all three (episode 20261009_111136: 254 frames, 254 rows, 254 steps). The replay inserts 55 steps per episode that have no real-video moment of their own (settle and close at the grasp, hold and rise after the release, a final 1 s wait); during them the real video and the flow hold still while the robot moves.

Who has it: the 100 kept episodes and 46 of the 65 failed ones (those whose replay ran; sim.npz success says how it ended). The other 19 failed episodes have no robot data: 17 have real-clock flow only (flow.npz; no grasp found in the hand track, or the hand was lost), 2 have nothing past the raw recording (MediaPipe hung).

Frames and clocks

  • Table frame: ArUco marker (id 0) at the origin, x away from the camera across the table, y left, z up, metres. Table axes = sim world axes.
  • Table → sim world: p_sim = p_table + sim_minus_real (sim.npz), one translation per episode that puts the pinch at the moment of grasp on the sim rim-grasp point. It does not put the mug flow on the sim cup (median 6.9 cm off, because I grasp the real rim at different spots). The sim cup always starts at (−0.11, 0); only the plate moves (cup + the real cup→plate offset).
  • Clocks: camera 30 fps; traj / retarget 20 Hz and flow.npz 10 Hz on the real clock; video_sim.mp4, flow_sim.npz and sim.npz 20 Hz on the robot's clock. t_real (in sim.npz and flow_sim.npz) is the real-video time each sim step follows; it holds during the inserted steps. All times are seconds since the first camera frame.
