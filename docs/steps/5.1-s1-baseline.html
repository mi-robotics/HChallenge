Success %, mean ± std over 5 seeds; each seed runs every scene 5 times (different flow-matching noise; the sim is deterministic), so 25 runs per scene and 3,650 runs per model (34 min on 8 workers). Closed loop in LIBERO: each run uses the scene its episode was recorded in, starts where that episode started, predicts 32 actions with 10 Euler steps, executes the first 10, and succeeds if the cup is on the plate after the episode length + 3 s. Flow models get that episode's recorded flow from the current sim step on (1.6 s, relative to the gripper unless noted), at every prediction. "failed" = the 46 scenes whose replay of my hand failed; no model trained on them, and flow models get the hand motion that failed. Trail = median gripper distance to the flow's pinch point; the recorded replay itself is 33 mm on test. All models use image augmentation and a learned position embedding on the encoder tokens unless noted.

  • Model: HVLA's flow-matching S1 (TheWisp/lerobot, Apache-2.0) with S2 and training-time RTC removed. DINOv2 ViT-S/14 (fine-tuned) on two cameras (third person + gripper, 256 → 224) and a state token (end-effector pose, finger positions) → transformer encoder → decoder predicting 32 actions (xyz move, gripper; 1.6 s). 93.5 M parameters. No language and no flow input.
  • Training: 80 train episodes (14,759 steps), all on the GPU; batch 64, AdamW, LR 2.5e-5 cosine to 2.5e-6, 20k steps, 54–56 min on the RTX 5090. img_aug: LeRobot's default image transforms (HVLA uses none): per image 3 of brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, sharpness, small affine, as batched GPU ops (0.02 s per batch).
  • Gripper camera re-recorded at 256 × 256 for all 146 replayed episodes before training (was 128); all 100 kept episodes succeeded again.
  • Evaluator check: replaying the recorded actions through the same setup reproduces success in 6/6 episodes.
