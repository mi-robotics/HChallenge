How the flow is made: the non-obvious choices

  • Table-frame metres, not pixels. Points are in the ArUco marker's frame, the same frame the sim uses, so a flow can drive the robot without knowing the camera.
  • 6 hand points, not 21. Thumb and index tips give the pinch (where the grasp is, and whether it is closed); wrist and 3 knuckles give the hand's orientation.
  • 16 steps at 10 Hz (1.5 s ahead). At the fastest hand speed (p95 0.39 m/s) a step is about 4 cm. Past the episode end the last step repeats.
  • Pixels from CoTracker, not MediaPipe. CoTracker covers MediaPipe's gaps (demo 5 has a 0.7 s gap where the hand swings about 10 cm). It is queried every 0.5 s from MediaPipe and each frame uses the nearest query: one query per video let the pinky knuckle drift 287 px.
  • Hand depth from the D405, at the tracked pixel. The step 1.6 pinch path agrees within a median 1–2 mm.
  • Hidden needs a depth check. CoTracker's "visible" says whether the pixel is visible, not the finger. MediaPipe's 3D hand, fitted to the D405 points, gives each point's expected depth. A point is hidden if something is more than 3 cm in front of it, it is more than 5 cm behind (off the hand), it has no depth, or it is within 10 px of the image edge.
  • Mug points come from a SAM 2 mask. SAM 2 segments the mug in every frame from one click; 8 points are spread over the first-frame mask. A mug point is hidden when CoTracker hides it or it leaves the mask. Points placed from the hand started off the mug in demo 4.
  • Mug depth comes from the hand, not the camera. The white mug gives no D405 depth. Each mug pixel's camera ray is met with a 4 cm-radius cylinder that moves with the pinch point, capped at the rim. Fitting the mug pose from its 8 points (PnP) drifted up to 11 cm along the line of sight during the carry, so it is only a check.
  • Each step averages ±50 ms. A step is the mean of the visible camera frames within 50 ms. Taking the nearest frame gave about twice the jitter.
  • Filling hidden steps. Hidden steps are filled only to avoid NaNs and keep visible = 0, so a model can mask them. Hand points are interpolated linearly. Mug points move with the visible mug points instead: linear filling drew them up to 32 cm off the mug.
